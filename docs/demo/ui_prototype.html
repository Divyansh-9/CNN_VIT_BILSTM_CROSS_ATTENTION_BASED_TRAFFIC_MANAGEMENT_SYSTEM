These are forecasts, not measurements. The vehicle number is the average of the counts across the next 60 seconds, which is why it can be a fraction; it counts vehicles in view, not vehicles passing through. Levels are set separately for each camera, so HIGH here is not the same amount of traffic as HIGH elsewhere. The level and the vehicle number are two separate forecasts, so they may not always match.

This system forecasts traffic conditions. It does not control physical traffic signals.

Technical details and research information

Display only: neither forecast is connected to signal timing. In the controller's state, 0.0 means an approach has no forecast and LOW is encoded as 1/3 (ADR-020 as amended, P110). Still open: P97a Part 2, the controller's behaviour under forecast faults, which is deferred.

No accuracy is shown here: a live score against the detector's own counts would be circular (ADR-014). Forecasters are persistence and ridge, not MFSTNet (ADR-027). No vehicles or video are shown (NFR-13). Forecasts count bicycles; the published vehicle counts do not (D1).

The edge reports itself through its heartbeat; the broker and the ingest do not report their own health, so a stream going stale is how a stopped one shows here.
