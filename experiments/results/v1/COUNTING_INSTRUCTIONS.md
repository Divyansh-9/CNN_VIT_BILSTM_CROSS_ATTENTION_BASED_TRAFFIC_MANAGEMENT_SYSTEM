# V1 counting instructions (draft v1; frozen after the inter-rater pilot)

**Read this once before you start.** Both labellers use these exact words. If
the pilot's agreement (ICC) is below 0.8, the instructions are revised
**once**, the pilot is repeated, and then they are frozen. Changes after the
freeze need a recorded amendment.

## Your task

Each item is one still frame from a traffic camera. **One region is
highlighted** with a coloured tint; the rest of the image is dimmed.

**Count the vehicles whose centre lies inside the highlighted region,** then
type the number and press **Next**.

## What counts as a vehicle

- **Counted:** cars, vans, trucks, buses, motorcycles and auto-rickshaws,
  **moving or stopped**.
- **Counted, if visible:** a vehicle partly hidden behind another, when you
  can see enough of it to tell it is a separate vehicle.
- **Not counted:**
  - pedestrians and bicycles;
  - parked vehicles clearly outside the carriageway (a driveway or a car
    park);
  - reflections;
  - vehicles printed on signs.

## Where the vehicle is

- **Judge by the vehicle's centre:** the middle of its visible body.
- **If the centre is inside the tinted region, count it.** If it is outside,
  do not, even if part of the vehicle overlaps the region.
- **If you genuinely cannot decide whether the centre is in or out,** count it
  only if more than half of the vehicle's visible body is inside.

## Rules for being consistent

- **Work alone.** Do not compare counts with the other labeller until both of
  you have exported.
- **There is no "right" answer to guess.** You will not see any model's count,
  and the frames are in random order.
- **If a frame is unusable** (black, frozen, or too blurred to count), enter
  **-1**. Do not estimate.
- **Take a short break every 20 minutes.** Fatigue changes counts.
- **When finished, press Export,** and send the file as it is. Do not edit it.

## What happens to your counts

They are compared with the other labeller's counts on a pilot of 40
lane-moments. Each lane-moment is 15 frames over one minute. Only then is the
main set counted.

**Your counts never leave the project team.** The footage is under an
unresolved licence and is used for internal evaluation only.
